About Our Project

TEAM TECH ROOT is a web-based system that simplifies garbage reporting. Every dustbin has a unique QR code. Users can report a full dustbin, overflowing garbage, bad smell, or damage. Authorised cleaning staff can view reports and update their status. Our goal is to make waste management easier and improve campus hygiene.

How It Works

📱

1. Scan QR Code

Scan the unique QR code attached to the dustbin using your phone.

📝

2. Report Problem

Select the problem and submit your report for that particular bin.

🧹

3. Staff Takes Action

Authorised cleaning staff review the report and update its status.
